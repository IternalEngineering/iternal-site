Website Build and Care Terms

Version 1.0 · Iternal Ltd

These terms set out how Iternal Ltd ("we", "us") will design, build and launch a website for your business ("you"), and look after it once it is live.

You accept these terms online, by ticking the acceptance box at the end of our sign-up process. When you do, these terms and the order details shown on that screen (your company name, the fees and the payment schedule) together form a binding agreement between us. We will email you a copy of both for your records.

1. The process

  • We begin with an in-depth conversation. One of our team, John, sits down with you to understand your business, your audience and what you want the site to do.
  • We design and build the site using our toolset, keeping you updated as it develops.
  • We handle the technical switchover, moving your domain and DNS across so everything points to the right place.
  • We launch. Once you have approved the site, we take it live.

2. What you get

  • A clean, succinct website, typically up to five pages, built around your conversation with John.
  • A modern, responsive design that works across desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Your content written up and laid out clearly.
  • Domain and DNS switchover handled for you.
  • One round of revisions before launch.
  • A live website that you own.

3. Looking after your site

The monthly care plan keeps your site secure, up to date and running smoothly. It includes:

  • Hosting and the day-to-day running of your website.
  • Software, security and maintenance updates.
  • Access to our web agent, which lets you make some updates to your site yourself, such as changing images or text.
  • A point of contact should any issues arise.

Larger pieces of work, such as new pages, a redesign or new functionality, sit outside the care plan and will be quoted separately.

4. Fees and payment

  • Build fee: £750, unless your order details state a different amount. 50% is payable on acceptance of these terms, and 50% on completion.
  • Care plan: £50 per month, unless your order details state a different amount, starting from the month after your site goes live.
  • All fees exclude VAT.
  • Invoices are payable within 14 days of receipt.
  • You own your domain. Any third-party domain registration or renewal fees are billed separately at cost.
